FORTNITE RACING FAQ.

What is the Full Send Racing island code?

0400-9013-9315. Enter it in Fortnite search to play.

Is Full Send Racing the official Rocket Racing?

No. Full Send Racing is an independent Fortnite Creative island by Dawman89, made for players who enjoy Rocket Racing-style arcade driving. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.

Is Full Send Racing a free Rocket Racing alternative?

Yes. It's free to access inside Fortnite and offers drifting, speedruns and competitive races on 15 tracks.

Does Full Send Racing have challenges and ranks?

Yes. The island features daily and weekly challenges, rank progression, personal bests and leaderboards.

How do I find other Fortnite racers?

Join the Full Send Racing Discord to find races, share clips and challenge other drivers.

How can I submit a world record?

Share your track, time, Epic display name and video or screenshot proof in our Discord for verification.
